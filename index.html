Everything you need to know about
Atlantis Paradise Island

A family-tested guide to towers, dining, Aquaventure, dolphins, and everything in between.

Plan your trip

A handful of things to lock in before your trip. The passport one matters most — start it now, even if your dates feel far away.

Passports

Required for every traveler, including infants. Apply 6+ months out; expedite if you're closer than that. If passports fall through, most travel insurance won't cover it as a cancellation reason — so start now, even if your dates feel far away.

Flights

Fly to Nassau (NAS). Most major US hubs offer direct service. Set a Google Flights alert for your dates to catch fare drops — and let me know if you'd like help comparing the options.

Airport transfers

About 30 minutes from NAS to Paradise Island. Not included with your stay unless bundled. Options range from a shared shuttle through private town car, SUV, or limo. Pre-book directly with Atlantis (1-800-ATLANTIS) or ask me to add transfers to your package.

Travel insurance

Strongly recommended. Covers trip cancellation for covered reasons, medical events while traveling, and baggage hiccups. Get a quote before your final trip payment — happy to help you compare.

Money matters

A few things to know: Atlantis is not all-inclusive. Your stay includes a daily resort fee (varies by tower — roughly $77 to $82.50/day including VAT) plus a per-person mandatory gratuity (ages 12+, scales with room category). On food and beverage, a service charge is added automatically. A credit-card hold is taken at check-in for incidentals — ask me for the current amount before you arrive. Atlantis also offers pre-paid dining credits with a bonus for buying ahead; ping me a few days before your trip if you want to lock those in.
